03 Story

From visual storytelling to AI transformation.

I began my career making complex ideas clear on a page. Ever since, I’ve been making them happen.

My career started in visual communication. Working on McKinsey & Company’s client presentations, I led a team of designers creating data-driven decks for global client reporting, and learned early that clarity is a delivery skill.

Over ten years at Quadwave Consulting I grew from Project Coordinator Trainee to Head of Delivery for Solution Consulting, and helped set up the PMO and the PM Centre of Excellence. At Deloitte I delivered programs for the UK member firm, then led the Digital Products workstream: 40+ people across five Scrum teams, a $3.6M agile portfolio, and generative AI built into how we planned and reported.

Today at Microsoft I drive enterprise AI transformation with Microsoft 365 Copilot. I lead discovery, adoption, governance and value realization, and partner with C-suite leaders across healthcare, banking and technology to turn AI strategy into measurable outcomes.
